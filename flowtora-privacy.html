Flowtora Studio

Privacy Policy and Terms · Last updated: 26 November 2026

Flowtora Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the Flowtora YouTube channel to manage that channel: uploading videos, setting thumbnails, managing playlists and comments, and reading channel analytics through the YouTube API Services. It is not offered to the public.

What data is accessed

With the channel owner's consent, the app accesses the owner's YouTube channel data (videos, playlists, comments, captions, analytics) via Google OAuth.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to perform the actions the channel owner requests. OAuth tokens and data are stored locally on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app's use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Terms

The app is provided as-is for the channel owner's personal use only.
